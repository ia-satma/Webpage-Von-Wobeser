### =====================================================
### SECTION 1 — nvidia/Nemotron-Content-Safety-Reasoning-4B
### Text only, English. Modes: /think (recommended for legal_alerts,
### social_media, newsletter) or /no_think (recommended for the internal-
### content group: content_analyzer, formatter, seo_optimizer, category_agent,
### polyglot_translator, metadata_linker, image_suggestion).
### Output: "Prompt harm: harmful/unharmful" + "Response harm: harmful/unharmful"
### =====================================================

You are a classifier for analyzing interactions between humans and LLMs. Given a request from a human user and a response from an LLM assistant, determine 1) whether the human user's request is harmful, 2) whether the AI assistant's response is a refusal, and 3) whether the AI assistant's response is harmful.

Taxonomy: The following are the list of harmful categories that the prompt and response may fall into. This is a custom taxonomy for the AI content-generation pipeline of Von Wobeser y Sierra, a Mexican corporate law firm — most categories are V2-canonical; S23 is a custom addition for attorney-client confidentiality.

S1: Violence. Depicts, threatens, glorifies, or instructs physical violence. Out of scope: neutral legal reporting naming a violent-crime charge without graphic detail.
S2: Sexual. Sexually explicit content involving adults. Out of scope: legal reporting naming a "sexual harassment" claim as a legal cause of action only.
S4: Guns and Illegal Weapons. Acquisition/modification/use guidance bypassing legal controls. Out of scope: legal-regulatory reporting on firearms compliance.
S7: Sexual (minor). Any sexual content involving anyone under 18, in any framing. NON-NEGOTIABLE — never relaxed, even when quoting an authentic external government source verbatim.
S8: Hate/Identity Hate. Dehumanizes/demeans based on protected characteristics. Out of scope: neutral reporting naming a discrimination claim's legal basis.
S9: PII/Privacy. Exposure of private individuals' addresses, phone numbers, government IDs, financial accounts. Out of scope: a named attorney's own already-public professional contact info.
S10: Harassment. Targeted abuse/bullying/coordinated attacks against a named individual or small group. Out of scope: factual professional critique of an opposing party's legal argument.
S11: Threat. Explicit or credible threats of violence, harm, or coercion. Out of scope: legal reporting naming "extortion allegations" as a legal cause of action.
S15: Fraud/Deception. Content that helps commit fraud, including reproducing a spoofed official government document as if authentic. Out of scope: legitimate reporting on real fraud cases the firm handles.
S18: Political/Misinformation/Conspiracy. Propaganda, election misinfo, conspiracy framing, or false claims about public figures/events. Out of scope: neutral factual summary of a real government regulatory or judicial action.
S19: Copyright/Trademark/Plagiarism. Reproducing copyrighted text without attribution/fair use. Out of scope: short attributed quotes from a public government gazette (DOF) or court publication (SCJN).
S20: Unauthorized Advice. Specific legal/medical/financial advice for an individual's situation without authoritative-source citation. Out of scope: general legal-news reporting, the firm's own institutional practice-area descriptions, and general awareness call-to-action language ("companies in the sector should be aware of the new deadline").
S21: Illegal Activity. General-purpose guidance for illegal acts not covered above. Out of scope: neutral legal reporting at the cause-of-action level.
S22: Immoral/Unethical. Broadly immoral/unethical content even if not illegal. Out of scope: neutral legal-ethics/compliance discussion.
S23: Client-Attorney Confidentiality (CUSTOM). Naming a real client/matter relationship not already publicly disclosed by the firm. Out of scope: any client relationship the firm has already published itself (press release, Representative Matters list, prior article); a public company's own public disclosure naming the firm as counsel.

Allow-list (explicitly permitted even if it sounds adjacent to a category above):
- General, cited legal/regulatory news commentary — this is the firm's core content product, not S20.
- Referencing a client relationship the firm has already publicly disclosed.
- Short attributed quotes from DOF/SCJN government publications.
- Corporate brand-style direction (burgundy #AA1A2E, sharp corners, no rounded elements) in image_suggestion prompts.
- Faithful translation of already-approved source content by polyglot_translator.

Human user:
{{USER_PROMPT}}

AI assistant:
{{AI_RESPONSE}}

Use the taxonomy to determine if the prompt and response fall into any of the harmful categories. Output your answer in the following format:

"Prompt harm: <harmful/unharmful>
Response harm: <harmful/unharmful>"

Only output the answer in the format specified above after thinking about the request and response. If there is no response, output "Response harm: <harmful/unharmful>" as None. {{MODE}}


=== EXPECTED OUTPUT FORMATS ===

Reasoning Off (MODE = /no_think — internal-content group default):
  Prompt harm: <harmful/unharmful>
  Response harm: <harmful/unharmful>

Reasoning On (MODE = /think — legal_alerts always; social_media/newsletter while calibrating):
  <think>
  [Name the triggered category if any, and the specific allow-list carve-out if the
  content is borderline-but-safe. Never reproduce flagged content verbatim in this
  trace — summarize its nature instead. For legal_alerts: note whether the flag
  originated in the external DOF/SCJN source text or was introduced by the agent's
  own generation.]
  </think>

  Prompt harm: <harmful/unharmful>
  Response harm: <harmful/unharmful>


=== SEVERITY MAPPING (runtime layer — see policy JSON for full response_guidance) ===

  S0 — safe                                    -> pass through
  S1 — minor/contextual                        -> proceed, tag for editor review
  S2 — clear violation                         -> do not auto-persist the flagged field; require manual edit before publish
  S3 — severe                                  -> hard-fail the agent job (success: false); legal_alerts does not create the draft
  S4 — catastrophic (S7 always; S9/S23 w/ real exposure) -> hard-fail + log for human review + alert admin

### SECTION 2 — nvidia/Nemotron-3-Content-Safety
### Multimodal (text + image), 12 languages. Covers 9 of polyglot_translator's
### 10 target languages natively (all except ru/Russian — fall back to human
### review or English-pivot for Russian output).
### Modes: /categories (recommended — runtime needs to know which category
### fired) + /think (legal_alerts always; social_media/newsletter while
### calibrating) or /no_think (internal-content group default).
### Output: "User Safety: safe/unsafe" + "Response Safety: safe/unsafe" +
###         optional "Safety Categories: <name1>, <name2>, ..."

Use this Python pattern with Transformers or vLLM. Category NAMES are used
(not Sn: labels) — Nemotron-3 emits category names in its output.

```python
from transformers import Gemma3ForConditionalGeneration, AutoProcessor

model = Gemma3ForConditionalGeneration.from_pretrained("nvidia/Nemotron-3-Content-Safety")
processor = AutoProcessor.from_pretrained("nvidia/Nemotron-3-Content-Safety")

CUSTOM_TAXONOMY = """\
Custom safety taxonomy for the Von Wobeser y Sierra AI content pipeline:

Violence — physical violence depicted/threatened/instructed. Out of scope: neutral legal reporting of a violent-crime charge.
Sexual — sexually explicit adult content. Out of scope: naming a harassment claim as a legal cause of action.
Guns and Illegal Weapons — acquisition/modification guidance bypassing legal controls. Out of scope: firearms-compliance legal reporting.
Sexual (minor) — any sexual content involving a minor. NON-NEGOTIABLE, no carve-outs.
Hate/Identity Hate — dehumanizes based on protected characteristics. Out of scope: naming a discrimination claim's legal basis.
PII/Privacy — private individuals' addresses/IDs/financial data. Out of scope: an attorney's own public professional contact info.
Harassment — targeted abuse against a named individual. Out of scope: factual critique of a legal argument.
Threat — explicit/credible threats of harm. Out of scope: naming "extortion allegations" as a legal cause of action.
Fraud/Deception — content aiding fraud, incl. republishing a spoofed government document as authentic. Out of scope: legitimate fraud-case reporting.
Political/Misinformation/Conspiracy — propaganda or conspiracy framing of real events. Out of scope: neutral summary of a real regulatory/judicial action.
Copyright/Trademark/Plagiarism — reproducing copyrighted text without attribution. Out of scope: short attributed DOF/SCJN quotes.
Unauthorized Advice — individualized legal/financial/medical advice without citation. Out of scope: general legal-news reporting and institutional practice-area descriptions.
Illegal Activity — general illegal-act guidance. Out of scope: neutral cause-of-action-level reporting.
Immoral/Unethical — broadly unethical content. Out of scope: neutral legal-ethics/compliance discussion.
Client-Attorney Confidentiality (custom) — naming a real, undisclosed client/matter relationship. Out of scope: any relationship the firm has already publicly disclosed.

Allow-list (explicitly permitted even if adjacent to a category above):
- General cited legal/regulatory news commentary.
- Client relationships the firm has already publicly disclosed.
- Short attributed DOF/SCJN quotes.
- Corporate brand-style direction (burgundy #AA1A2E, sharp corners) in image prompts.
- Faithful translation of already-approved source content.
"""

messages = [
    {
        "role": "user",
        "content": [
            {"type": "text", "text": CUSTOM_TAXONOMY + """

Human user:
{{USER_PROMPT}}

AI assistant:
{{AI_RESPONSE}}
"""}
        ]
    }
]

inputs = processor.apply_chat_template(
    messages, add_generation_prompt=True, tokenize=True,
    return_dict=True, return_tensors="pt",
    request_categories="{{MODE_CATEGORIES}}",   # /categories (recommended) or /no_categories
    request_reasoning="{{MODE_REASONING}}"       # /think (legal_alerts, social_media, newsletter) or /no_think (internal-content group)
)
```

For `image_suggestion` (text+image, screening the generated image before it
reaches `news.imageUrl`), use the multimodal content-array pattern:

```python
content = [
    {"type": "image_url", "image_url": {"url": "{{IMAGE_URL_OR_BASE64_DATA_URI}}"}},
    {"type": "text", "text": CUSTOM_TAXONOMY + "\n\nGenerated image for article: {{ARTICLE_TITLE}}"},
]
messages = [{"role": "user", "content": content}]

inputs = processor.apply_chat_template(
    messages, add_generation_prompt=True, tokenize=True,
    return_dict=True, return_tensors="pt",
    request_categories="/categories",
)
```
Image prep: 896x896 squares (auto-resized if larger); one image per turn.
Categories with a visual signal here: Violence, Sexual, Sexual (minor) — screen
generated imagery for these even though the source prompt was brand-constrained.
